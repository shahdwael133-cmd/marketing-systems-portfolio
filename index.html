Signature achievements

  • Engineered an autonomous content production pipeline. Five scheduled cloud agents produce 3 videos, 2 post carousels and 1 editorial carousel with captions every week, then consolidate the entire output into a single approval email.
  • Built a self-governing performance intelligence dashboard. It integrates 15 data feeds across 7 platforms, benchmarks every 14-day period against the previous one, writes its own analysis and reports to leadership every 2 weeks. A built-in data integrity check halts publication and issues a diagnostic report whenever a source is stale.
  • Established a competitive intelligence program with zero tool budget. Four global competitors are monitored every 2 weeks across LinkedIn, Reddit, review platforms, website changes, paid advertising and YouTube, on Apify's free plan with a hard $1 cost ceiling per run.
  • Shipped a proprietary 3D CAD viewer as a lead generation asset. Engineers inspect their own STEP files directly in the browser, beside a quote request, targeting a search term with roughly 5,400 US searches a month.
  • Converted content performance data into an evidence-based creative formula. A diagnostic across 128 posts isolated what the top performers shared: 31 to 65 second runtimes, Egyptian Arabic captions and a real team member on camera.
  • Re-architected a failing system for resilience. When the first weekly content run, hosted locally, failed 3 times unattended, I migrated it to cloud infrastructure and validated that its output matched the original within 1.7%.

Engineering principles

  1. End-to-end autonomy. Every automation runs from data intake to final delivery. No manual steps hidden in the middle.
  2. Verified before trusted. Each system passes a full dry run and ships with a written operating manual.
  3. Acceptance criteria first. Visual work begins with written pass/fail standards, then is screenshot and scored against them.
  4. Conversion by design. Every content asset ends in a single commercial action: upload a drawing or request a quote.
  5. Factual integrity. Real photography and verified claims only. No fabricated statistics and no synthetic people.
  6. Cost discipline. Free tiers first. Any paid process is validated at minimum scale before full volume.
